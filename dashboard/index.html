How to use it

Tilt
Move the cursor. Aim at the middle to stop.
Tap
Click. Thumb onto the side of your index finger.
Tap twice
Double-click. Leave about half a second between taps.
Sweep
Scroll. Move the whole hand up or down; sweep again quickly to go further.
Palm up
Hold for a second to pause or resume steering. Right after Start it just confirms steering is on.
